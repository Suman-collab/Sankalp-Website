FAQs

Answers to common queries regarding Sankalp participation and campus rules.

Sankalp is open to all college students across India! Whether you are from NIST University or any other recognized institution, if you have a passion for cultural events, technical competitions, hackathons, or esports, you are warmly invited to participate.

Sankalp features a diverse spectrum of events including cultural performances (dance, music, drama), technical competitions (coding, robotics, web designing, cloud hackathons), sports and LAN tournaments, flagship 2K26 showcases, and celebrity star nights.

Students and teams can participate by connecting with the respective student club coordinators listed on the Clubs page or by reaching out to the student symposium committee at the NIST University campus.

Yes, NIST University provides on-campus hostel accommodation and cafeteria meals for verified outstation participants throughout the festival duration. Please reach out to the festival helpdesk on arrival.
