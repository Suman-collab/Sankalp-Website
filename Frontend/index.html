FAQs

Answers to common queries regarding Sankalp participation and campus rules.

Sankalp is open to all college students across India! Whether you are from NIST University or any other recognized institution, if you have a passion for cultural events, technical competitions, hackathons, or esports, you are warmly invited to participate.

Sankalp features a diverse spectrum of events including cultural performances (dance, music, drama), technical competitions (coding, robotics, web designing, cloud hackathons), sports and LAN tournaments, fashion showcases (Mr. & Miss Sankalp), and celebrity star nights.

You can register directly on this website by heading over to the Events page, choosing your competition, and clicking Register. You can also contact the designated club representatives listed on the Clubs page.

Yes, NIST University provides on-campus hostel accommodation and cafeteria meals for verified outstation participants throughout the festival duration. Please select the accommodation option during registration.
